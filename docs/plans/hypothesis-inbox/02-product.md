# Gate 1 — Product

**Problem**: a refused hypothesis vanished from view. To the owner that reads as "the system ate
my idea", when in fact it recorded the idea with a precise reason (usually a feature the engine
cannot derive yet).

**Outcome**: `/strategies` shows every recorded hypothesis: held ones first with an amber
HELD · status chip, authored ones with "→ strategy-name". Counts on top. A refresh control.
Empty state explains that refusals are held with reasons, not discarded.

**Success evidence**: e2e posts a hypothesis the triage must hold (market-cap + sentiment —
underivable quantities), sees it in the inbox flagged HELD, and sees it survive a refresh.

**Non-goals**: editing/re-triaging from the inbox; notification badges; pagination (the list is
small; add when it measurably is not).
